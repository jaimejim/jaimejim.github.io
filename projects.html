Projects

A handful of side projects I maintain outside work. Mostly small things I build for myself, my family, or to test an idea end-to-end. Work at Ericsson lives elsewhere.

01 · Agentic systems

  • ad.jaime.win

    Agent Directory

    Live instance of an IETF draft I wrote on agent registration and discovery. Register agents, search the directory, drive it from another agent via its skill file.

  • camara.jaime.win

    Camara Sandbox

    A sandbox for calling the CAMARA network APIs from an LLM agent. 97 telco APIs exposed as two MCP tools, so you can try mobile network capabilities in plain language.

  • tertulia.jaime.win

    Tertulia

    Chat over a YouTube corpus. Each video is distilled locally into a structured note with topic links, and questions are answered by walking the graph. Around 200 videos and 660 topics so far, served from a single Cloudflare Worker.

  • coap agents

    Agentic IoT over CoAP

    Wiring LLM agents to real constrained devices over CoAP. Demos, notes, and an Internet-Draft on the architecture.

02 · Home & kids

  • mates.jaime.win

    Mates

    Maths practice site I built for my kids. No accounts, runs on any tablet.

  • recetario.jaime.win

    Recetario

    Our family recipe book, in Spanish. Just the recipes, no ads, no tracking.

03 · Offline

  • capperi.fi

    Capperi

    Two wood-fired Napoletana pizzerias in Helsinki that I co-own with friends. A side project, in the non-software sense.
