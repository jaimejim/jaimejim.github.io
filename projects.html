Projects

A handful of side projects I maintain outside work. Mostly small things I build for myself, my family, or to test an idea end-to-end. Work at Ericsson lives elsewhere.

01 · Agentic systems

  • ad.jaime.win

    Agent Directory

    Live instance of an IETF draft I wrote on agent registration and discovery. You can register your own agents and search for others. There's also a small skill file so another agent can drive it.

  • camara.jaime.win

    Camara Sandbox

    A small sandbox for exploring the CAMARA network APIs with an LLM agent. Useful for seeing what mobile network capabilities look like when you can call them in plain language.

  • tertulia.jaime.win

    Tertulia

    A chat interface over a YouTube corpus. Each video gets distilled locally into a structured note with topic links, and questions are answered by walking that graph. Around 200 videos and a few hundred topics so far, all served from a single Cloudflare Worker.

  • coap agents

    Agentic IoT over CoAP

    Experiments connecting LLM agents to real constrained IoT devices over CoAP, the protocol most of them actually speak. Small demos, some notes, and an Internet-Draft describing the pattern.

02 · Home & kids

  • mates.jaime.win

    Mates

    A small maths practice site I built for my kids. Works on any tablet or phone, no accounts or sign-ups, just open it and start.

  • recetario.jaime.win

    Recetario

    Our family recipe book, in Spanish. Just the recipes, cleanly laid out, with no ads or tracking.

03 · Offline

    Capperi

    Two wood-fired Napoletana pizzerias in Helsinki that I co-own with friends. Very much a side project, just not a software one.
