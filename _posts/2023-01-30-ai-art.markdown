After the ChatGPT post I kept experimenting with AI tools, this time for image generation. There are many tools to try: ChatGPT Image Generation, Lexica Art, Dream Studio (Stable Diffusion) and Midjourney.

More advanced users on Twitter, like [@dymokomi](https://twitter.com/dymokomi), make real art with these tools. They start from an idea, a texture, a seed image and a style, then refine through trial and error.

I will not cover how to use each tool. This post shows some images I generated with simple Midjourney prompts, a few textures and little editing.
