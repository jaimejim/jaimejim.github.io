🇱🇻 Cēsis Easter 2026

Family of 5 · Ferry + car · 4 nights · Thu Apr 2 — Mon Apr 6

⛴ Day 1 — Helsinki → Tallinn → Cēsis
Thu Apr 2Morning ferry Helsinki → Tallinn (~2h). Drive Tallinn → Cēsis (~3.5h, 300 km via E67). Quick Tallinn old town stop if timing works. Settle in by evening.
🏰 Day 2 — Cēsis Old Town
Fri Apr 3Medieval Castle candlelight tour with lanterns 🕯. Walk the old town: St. John's Church (climb the tower), Maija Park along the moat. Afternoon: Cēsis Brewery (oldest in the Baltics, 1590). Dinner at Cafe Popular or Vinetas un Allas Kārumlāde.
🌲 Day 3 — Gauja National Park
Sat Apr 4Gauja NP hike: sandstone cliffs, river valley trails. Līgatne Nature Trails: forest enclosures with brown bears, lynx, elk, wild boar (~20 min from Cēsis). Afternoon: Āraišu Lake Fortress (reconstructed Iron Age lake dwelling). Evening: relax at accommodation.
🏔 Day 4 — Sigulda Day Trip
Sun Apr 5Drive to Sigulda (30 min). Cable car across Gauja valley. Turaida Castle + sculpture garden. Gūtmaņa Cave (largest in Baltics, inscriptions from 1668). Bobsled track for the brave. Easter lunch in Sigulda.
⛴ Day 5 — Cēsis → Tallinn → Helsinki
Mon Apr 6Morning drive to Tallinn (~3.5h). Quick old town stop if time. Afternoon/evening ferry home to Helsinki.
