# Cēsis Easter Trip 2026

## Overview
Family trip (5 pax) to Cēsis, Latvia over Easter weekend.

## Key dates
- **Easter 2026**: Sun Apr 5 (Good Friday Apr 3, Easter Monday Apr 6)
- **Travel window**: Thu Apr 2 - Mon Apr 6 (4 nights)

## Route
1. **Helsinki → Tallinn** by ferry (~2h, with car)
   - Tallink or Viking Line, book car + cabin
   - Depart morning Thu Apr 2
2. **Tallinn → Cēsis** by car (~3.5h, ~300 km via E67/A3)
   - Optional: quick Tallinn old town stop if timing works
3. **Return**: Mon Apr 6, reverse

## Map
```
Helsinki ──ferry 2h──▶ Tallinn ──drive 3.5h──▶ Cēsis
                         │                       │
```

## Schedule sketch
| Day | Plan |
|-----|------|
| Thu Apr 2 | Morning ferry, drive to Cēsis, settle in |
| Fri Apr 3 | Medieval castle (candlelight tour), old town |
| Sat Apr 4 | Gauja NP hike, Līgatne Nature Trails (bears, lynx, elk) |
| Sun Apr 5 | Day trip to Sigulda: cable car, bobsled, Turaida Castle |
| Mon Apr 6 | Drive back to Tallinn, afternoon/evening ferry home |

## Things to do
- **Cēsis Medieval Castle**: explore by candlelight with lanterns
- **Gauja National Park**: hiking, forest trails
- **Līgatne Nature Trails**: bears, lynx, elk in forest enclosures
- **Sigulda** (30 min from Cēsis): cable car over the valley, bobsled track, Turaida Castle
- **Artisan fair**: hands-on medieval crafts for kids (check dates closer)

## Accommodation options
- **Villa Santa**: 4-star in the national park, ~€160/night
- **Hotel Tigra**: pool, parking, ~€62/night
- **Estere**: self-catering cottage, garden, quiet
