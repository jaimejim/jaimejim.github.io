I'm a researcher originally from Spain and currently based in Helsinki, working at Ericsson Research. My focus is on **Applied AI, IoT, and Web Standards**, where I enjoy bridging early ideas and real-world applications.

Over the past 15 years at Ericsson, I've had the opportunity to contribute to various efforts; from prototyping technologies to helping shape standards. I currently co-chair the IETF CoRE Working Group and have co-authored device management specifications used in millions of connected devices. I've also been fortunate to receive the 2025 Ericsson Key Contributor Award and hold around 80 patents.

I'm passionate about solving complex technical problems and finding practical ways to make technology more accessible. When I'm not working on standards or research, I enjoy experimenting with productivity tools and sharing knowledge with the community through writing and speaking.

Outside of tech, my wife and I are raising three kids together. I co-own two pizzerias in Helsinki and enjoy staying active through swimming and padel. You'll often find me at Allas Seapool in Helsinki.

I speak Spanish, English, Italian, and German.
