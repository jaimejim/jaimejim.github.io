I’m a researcher originally from Spain, now based in Helsinki, working at Ericsson Research. My focus is on **Applied AI, IoT, and Web Standards**, where I enjoy bridging early ideas and real-world applications.

Over the past 15 years at Ericsson, I’ve had the opportunity to contribute to various efforts—from prototyping technologies to helping shape standards. I currently co-chair the IETF CoRE Working Group and have co-authored device management specifications used in millions of connected devices. I’ve also been fortunate to receive the 2025 Ericsson Key Contributor Award and hold around 80 patents.

I speak Spanish, English, Italian, and German.
