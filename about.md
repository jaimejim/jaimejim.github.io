<h1 id="about">About</h1>

<p><img class="portrait" src="{{ site.url }}/{{ site.picture }}" width="144" height="144" alt="Jaime Jiménez"></p>

I'm a researcher currently based in Helsinki, working at [Ericsson Research](https://www.ericsson.com/en/ericsson-research). My focus is on **Applied AI, IoT, and Web Standards**, where I enjoy bridging early ideas and real-world applications.

At Ericsson I've worked from prototyping to helping shape standards. I currently co-chair the [IETF CoRE Working Group](https://datatracker.ietf.org/wg/core/about/) and have co-authored device management specifications used in millions of connected devices. I've also been fortunate to receive the **2025 Ericsson Key Contributor Award** and hold around 80 patents.

I'm passionate about solving complex technical problems and finding practical ways to make technology more accessible. When I'm not working on standards or research, I enjoy experimenting with productivity tools and sharing knowledge with the community through writing and speaking.

Outside of tech, my wife and I are raising three kids together. I co-own two [Capperi](https://capperi.fi/) pizzerias in Helsinki and enjoy staying active through swimming and padel. You'll often find me at [Allas Seapool](https://allasseapool.fi/) in Helsinki.

I speak Spanish, English, Italian, and German.
